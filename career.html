01 Modern One-Page Resume



```text
Write a one-page resume for a [ROLE] with [X] years in [INDUSTRY].
Structure:
- Header: name, title, location, links
- Experience: 3 roles, 3 bullets each, action verb + outcome +
  number
- Skills: 8 relevant hard skills
- Education
No first person, no objective statement, no photo.
Under 450 words. Bullet length under 22 words.
```

02 ATS-Optimised Resume



```text
Rewrite this resume to pass ATS for this job:
[PASTE RESUME]
[PASTE JOB DESCRIPTION]
Rules:
- Mirror exact keywords and phrases from the JD
- Standard headings: Summary, Experience, Education, Skills
- No tables, columns, text boxes, headers or footers
- No images, graphics or unusual fonts
- Quantify every bullet possible
- Under 500 words
Then list the keywords you matched.
```

03 Career Change Resume



```text
Write a resume for someone moving from [CURRENT ROLE] to
[TARGET ROLE].
- Lead with transferable skills, not job titles
- Reframe achievements in the target industry's language
- Summary that owns the change honestly
- 3 bullets per role showing relevance
Under 450 words.
```

04 Senior / Executive Resume



```text
Write an executive resume for a [C-LEVEL ROLE] in [INDUSTRY].
- Lead with scale: team size, revenue, budget, markets
- Strategic impact over task lists
- Board-ready formatting with clean sections
- 3-4 roles, 4 bullets each
- Board and advisory section
Under 550 words. No bullet longer than 25 words.
```

05 Graduate / Entry Level Resume



```text
Write a resume for a new graduate applying to [ROLE] with limited
experience.
- Lead with education, projects and relevant modules
- Turn coursework and part-time work into achievements
- Skills section weighted toward tools
- Honest about experience level, confident about capability
Under 400 words.
```

06 Technical / Engineer Resume



```text
Write a technical resume for a [ENGINEERING ROLE].
- Skills section with proficiency grouping
- Projects with stack, scale and outcome
- Impact bullets with metrics (latency, users, cost)
- No soft-skill filler bullets
- Keyword-rich for ATS
Under 500 words.
```

07 Resume Quantification Pass



```text
Here is my resume:
[PASTE RESUME]
Rewrite EVERY bullet that lacks a number. Infer plausible metrics
where reasonable, mark them [CONFIRM] so I can verify, and never
invent a figure that could misrepresent me.
Before/after comparison for the 5 weakest bullets.
```

08 Resume Bullet Rewriter



```text
Rewrite these weak bullets into strong ones:
[PASTE BULLETS]
Formula: strong action verb + what you did + measurable result.
Cut every bullet over 22 words. Remove: "responsible for",
"helped with", "worked on". Show 3 options for each.
```

09 Resume Length & Trim Check



```text
Here is my resume:
[PASTE RESUME]
Tell me: word count, page estimate at 10pt, which section to cut,
the 3 least valuable bullets, and what's missing entirely.
Be ruthless - hiring managers spend 7 seconds.
```

10 Job-Specific Tailoring



```text
Here is my master resume:
[PASTE MASTER RESUME]
Here is the job:
[PASTE JOB DESCRIPTION]
Produce a tailored version. Reorder experience to put the most
relevant first, rewrite the summary for this exact role, add
missing keywords, cut irrelevant content. Under 450 words.
```

11 Cover Letter (specific Company)



```text
Write a cover letter for [ROLE] at [COMPANY].
- Paragraph 1: why THIS company - name something real
- Paragraph 2: your most relevant achievement, with a number
- Paragraph 3: what you'd do in the first 90 days
- Paragraph 4: confident close without desperation
Under 300 words. Never open with "I am writing to express...".
```

12 Cover Letter (career Change)



```text
Write a cover letter for someone moving from [OLD FIELD] to
[NEW FIELD].
- Own the change directly in paragraph 1
- Frame the old career as an advantage, not baggage
- One concrete transferable achievement
- Under 300 words.
```

13 Short Cover Letter (250 Words)



```text
Write a 250-word max cover letter for [ROLE].
Every sentence must earn its place. No throat-clearing.
```

14 Linkedin Headline (10 Options)



```text
Write 10 LinkedIn headline options for a [ROLE] in [INDUSTRY].
- Under 80 characters each (show count)
- Lead with the value delivered, not the job title
- One searchable keyword recruiters use
- No emoji spam, no "| Speaker | Visionary |"
```

15 Linkedin About Section



```text
Write a LinkedIn About section for a [ROLE] in [INDUSTRY].
- Line 1: a hook stating the value you bring
- 3 short paragraphs
- One specific result with a number
- First person, conversational
- End with a clear invitation to connect
Under 180 words.
```

16 Linkedin Featured / Experience Descriptions



```text
Write descriptions for 3 of my roles:
[PASTE ROLES]
Each: 2 short paragraphs, achievement-led, no duty lists, with a
number in each. Under 60 words per role.
```

17 Linkedin Skills & Endorsements



```text
Pick the 10 strongest skills for a [ROLE] application from:
[PASTE SKILLS LIST]

why each matters for that role.
```
