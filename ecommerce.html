Ecommerce, Amazon & Shopify Growth

Keyword research, listing copy, pricing and retention for online stores. **18 free prompts below.** The complete pack — and the other 4,000+ prompts across 164 categories — lives in the [Dreamer Prompt Lab store](https://www.getly.store/store/dreamer-prompt-lab-muifdken).

01 Primary Keyword Pick



```text
Identify the single strongest primary keyword for [PRODUCT] in the [NICHE] category on Amazon. Weigh search volume, purchase intent, and competition level, then recommend one head term plus three long-tail backups. Return a ranked list with a one-line justification per term, under 120 words, practical tone.
```

02 Title Formula Variations



```text
Write four Amazon product titles for [PRODUCT] aimed at [AUDIENCE], each built on a different formula: keyword-first, benefit-first, problem-first, and brand-first. Keep every title under 200 characters, front-load the term [KEYWORD], and avoid promotional words such as "best" or "number one". Label each option.
```

03 Long-Tail Keyword Harvest



```text
List 25 long-tail buyer keywords for [PRODUCT] that a shopper would type just before purchasing. Group them into informational, comparison, and ready-to-buy buckets. Include exact-phrase spellings only, no invented terms, and mark the five with strongest buying intent. Output as a plain table.
```

04 Competitor Title Teardown



```text
Analyze this competitor title: [PASTE TITLE]. Identify the keywords used, the order of elements, missing benefit language, and characters wasted. Then rewrite it stronger for [PRODUCT] using the same target keyword set. Keep the rewrite under 200 characters and explain your changes in three bullets.
```

05 Search Term Mapping



```text
Build a search term map for [PRODUCT]. Assign one primary keyword, eight secondary keywords, and twenty supporting backend terms. Ensure no phrase repeats across sections, stay within Amazon's byte limits, and exclude brand names you do not own. Present as three clearly labeled lists.
```

06 Category-Specific Title Rules



```text
Draft an Amazon title for [PRODUCT] in the [CATEGORY] category that respects that category's naming conventions and required attributes. Include size, count, material, and color where relevant, keep it under 180 characters, and place the main keyword [KEYWORD] in the first 80 characters.
```

07 Brand Name Placement



```text
Decide whether the brand name [BRAND] should appear at the start or end of the title for [PRODUCT], and justify the choice for brand-registered versus non-branded listings. Provide two final title examples under 200 characters each, keeping the primary keyword in a high-impact position.
```

08 Character Limit Maximizer



```text
Here is my current title: [PASTE TITLE]. Optimize it to use every available character without keyword stuffing or awkward repetition. Preserve the strongest terms, add one high-value missing modifier, and keep it under 200 characters. Return the revised title plus a word-by-word rationale in under 100 words.
```

09 Shopping Intent Keywords



```text
Generate 15 Amazon search phrases for [PRODUCT] with clear transactional intent, such as "with", "for", "set of", and "bulk". Exclude purely informational queries. Sort by likely conversion value and note the modifier pattern each one reveals about the [AUDIENCE] buyer.
```

10 Seasonal Keyword Injection



```text
Rewrite the title and first two bullet points for [PRODUCT] to capture [SEASON] and [HOLIDAY] shoppers without breaking evergreen keyword coverage. Keep the title under 200 characters, add two seasonal terms only, and return both a seasonal version and a permanent version.
```

11 Locale-Specific Title



```text
Create an Amazon listing title for [PRODUCT] targeting the [COUNTRY] marketplace, translating key terms into [LANGUAGE] while keeping the English brand and model number intact. Keep it under 200 characters, note any cultural phrasing risks, and list three keywords unique to that marketplace.
```

12 Keyword Negative List



```text
Compile a negative keyword list for [PRODUCT]: 20 terms that trigger irrelevant traffic or violate Amazon policy, including competitor brands, unrelated meanings of [KEYWORD], and unowned trademark terms. Format as a two-column table with the term and the reason for exclusion.
```

13 Broad And Exact Balance



```text
Split my keyword set for [PRODUCT] into exact-match terms to defend and broad-match terms to test. Use this list: [PASTE KEYWORDS]. Return two labeled groups of ten, a one-line rationale per group, and a suggested starting budget split of 70/30 between them.
```

14 Title Readability Score



```text
Score this Amazon title from 1 to 10 for clarity, grammar, and scannability: [PASTE TITLE]. Flag awkward capitalization, filler words, and redundant modifiers, then rewrite it for [AUDIENCE] so a shopper understands the product in three seconds. Keep the rewrite under 200 characters.
```

15 Dimension And Spec Words



```text
List the specification keywords shoppers use for [PRODUCT], such as size, weight, capacity, wattage, material, and pack count, that must appear in the title or bullets. Output as a checklist with the exact value for [PRODUCT] and where each should be placed in the listing.
```

16 Gift Occasion Keywords



```text
Write 12 gift-related search terms for [PRODUCT] covering occasions like [OCCASION], birthdays, and housewarmings. Then add one title variation and one bullet hook that use the top three terms naturally. Keep the tone warm but not cheesy, under 60 words per piece of copy.
```

17 Problem-Aware Keywords



```text
Identify 15 pain-point search phrases that signal a shopper with [PROBLEM] could buy [PRODUCT]. Group them by urgency level. Rewrite the title's benefit clause so it speaks to the highest-urgency problem using no more than 12 additional words.
```

18 Substitute Product Keywords



```text
Name 10 alternative products or methods shoppers compare against [PRODUCT], then craft two title modifiers and three bullets that position [PRODUCT] as the better choice without naming competitors directly. Keep all copy policy-safe and under 200 characters for the title.
```
